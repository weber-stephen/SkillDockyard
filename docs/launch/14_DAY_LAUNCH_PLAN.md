# 14-day soft launch plan

> Superseded for acquisition sequencing by [the complete marketing plan](MARKETING_PLAN.md), which excludes LinkedIn at the founder's request.

| Day | Owner | Action | Time | Success metric | Dependency |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Codex | Merge tested funnel, SEO, and docs work through PR | 30m | CI passes | Founder approves PR/preview |
| 2 | Founder | Configure GA4 in existing GTM; validate consented events | 45m | DebugView receives safe events | GTM access |
| 3 | Founder | Complete one verified signup → starter-skill journey | 30m | First-value path works | Production access |
| 4 | Founder | Send 5 personalized feedback requests | 30m | 2 replies | Warm contacts |
| 5 | Founder | Publish one disclosed LinkedIn workflow post | 30m | 3 qualified visits | Founder approval |
| 6 | Founder | Review funnel and support/error signals | 30m | No critical breakage | GA4/Sentry access |
| 7 | Codex | Triage observed friction | 30m | Ranked issue list | Founder notes |
| 8 | Founder | Send 10 more targeted feedback requests | 45m | 5 total replies | Contacts |
| 9 | Founder | Verify one community’s rules and contribute value | 30m | One compliant contribution | Moderator/rule check |
| 10 | Codex | Improve the weakest observed funnel step | 60m | Regression tests pass | Real feedback |
| 11 | Founder | Follow up with voluntary interested users | 30m | 2 product conversations | Consent/interest |
| 12 | Founder | Test one headline or post angle | 30m | Tagged visits/signups | UTM links |
| 13 | Codex | Summarize directional learning | 30m | Updated experiment status | Analytics data |
| 14 | Founder | Choose channel to double down on next | 30m | Next experiment selected | 14-day review |

External posting, outreach, analytics configuration, and deployment remain founder actions. Do not claim any task complete without evidence.
