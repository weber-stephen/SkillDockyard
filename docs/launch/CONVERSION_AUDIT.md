## Funnel

Discovery should lead to the campaign page or homepage, then the live Campaign Brief Builder demo, then free signup, email verification, and the starter-skill path. First value is reached only when a verified account adds or imports its first real skill. Demo use is useful education but not activation.

The signup form retains required Terms, Privacy, CAPTCHA, and confirmation safeguards. Those are intentional security and legal controls, not friction to remove.
