# Independent AI SaaS marketing review

**Reviewed:** [30-day marketing plan](MARKETING_PLAN.md), by a separate AI SaaS marketing specialist agent. Recommendations are directional; no user demand, community access, or launch results have been established by this review.

## Highest-priority recommendations

1. **Validate the buyer, not just the builder.** Arrange 3–5 voluntary conversations with marketing users, subject to founder approval. Ask whether they use Codex or Claude Code, whether coworkers share skills, and what breaks in that handoff. Technical-community interest alone does not validate the marketing-team segment. **Incorporated:** early interviews and segment-specific evidence in the plan.
2. **Treat silence as inconclusive.** A blocked post, low exposure, or no replies is not evidence that the problem does not exist. Reframe only after relevant people provide substantive counterevidence. **Incorporated:** experiment decisions and weekly evidence rubric.
3. **Disclose the founder connection even without a link.** A research question can still be connected to a product. Contribute only where the community permits and avoid disguised pitches. **Incorporated:** marketing-community discussion draft.
4. **Preflight the real demo and measurement.** Test meaningful anonymous interaction, reset behavior, mobile, signup and confirmation, privacy, and support. The documented email-confirmation mismatch could undercount signup completions; actual production behavior is not verified. Consent and deduplication limitations prevent assuming a reliable first-value user count. **Incorporated:** Show HN gate, funnel caveats, and launch checklist.
5. **Keep the promise precise.** Say teammates can check for and install updates, not that skills synchronize automatically. **Incorporated:** Product Hunt draft.
6. **Protect founder time and identity.** Include outreach and responses in the 30–60 minute daily budget, reserve time after a Show HN launch, and review identity exposure on every platform—not just LinkedIn. **Incorporated:** schedule, launch preflight, and constraints.
