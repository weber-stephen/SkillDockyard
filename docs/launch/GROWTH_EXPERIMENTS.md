# Growth experiments

| Hypothesis | Stage | Success threshold | Minimum observation | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign-specific hero improves signup intent | Landing → signup | 10 qualified signup starts from 100 campaign-page visitors | 14 days | Continue if directional lift vs homepage |
| Starter skills improve activation | Signup → first value | 40% of confirmed signups add a first skill | 14 days | Iterate onboarding if below threshold |
| Warm feedback outreach yields qualified users | Acquisition | 5 activated users from 25 relevant conversations | 7 days | Continue if feedback is specific and repeatable |
| LinkedIn workflow post reaches the right audience | Acquisition | 5 tagged signup starts and 2 feedback replies | 7 days | Iterate angle or stop |
| Review-before-share message resonates | Activation → retention | 3 teams submit an update or invite a collaborator | 21 days | Continue or defer |

Treat all results as directional at this sample size. Record source labels in approved UTMs and do not claim statistical significance.
