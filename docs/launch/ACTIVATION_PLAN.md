# Activation plan

## First-value path

1. Verify email and enter the personal workspace.
2. Choose **Start with campaign examples**.
3. Add the editable Campaign Brief Builder starter skill.
4. Open it, tailor the workflow, and later submit it when ready to share.

Activation is a verified account with its first persisted skill. The first meaningful team outcome is a reviewed, published campaign skill; treat that as a deeper retention indicator, not the initial activation bar.

Ask activated users: “What made it easy or hard to turn this into a workflow your team would reuse?” Collect replies manually through the founder; do not add unsolicited email automation.
