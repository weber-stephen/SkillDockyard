# Direction

## Open Workshop

Open Workshop frames Skill Dockyard as a welcoming place where useful team practices are shaped together. Larger touch targets, soft but disciplined geometry, an asymmetric metrics workspace, and warm material colors make review feel collaborative without weakening ownership, status, or publishing controls.

## Brand Attributes

Approachable, capable, collaborative, warm, practical, and optimistic.

## Major Changes

- Widened and softened the navigation into a clear workspace rail with stronger active context.
- Introduced a warm cream, sage, forest, straw, and restrained clay palette.
- Rebuilt dashboard metrics as an asymmetric workbench with one dominant workspace total.
- Grouped the active submission on a warm attention surface rather than a warning-like alert.
- Differentiated lifecycle steps as connected work zones instead of repeated white cards.
- Increased control height and focus visibility across forms and actions.
- Added responsive metric and table-record layouts.
- Updated key interface terminology to match the product language rules.

## Design System

- Typography: Avenir Next Rounded where available, falling back to Avenir Next; strong friendly headings with highly legible body copy.
- Color: cream canvas, sage navigation, forest actions, straw review surfaces, clay used sparingly for supporting zones.
- Spacing: generous touch targets, medium-density content, and roomy section separation.
- Surfaces: matte colored work zones with minimal shadow and no glass effects.
- Radius: 7px to 12px, reserved for interactive and grouped regions.
- Motion: 180ms deceleration, subtle scale feedback, and a tactile pressed primary navigation action.
- Focus: three-pixel green focus ring with offset.

## UX Changes

The dashboard emphasizes the size of the shared skill collection, while review and submission counts remain adjacent. Controls are easier to target, and mobile tables become friendly labeled records. All permission, submission, publishing, and routing behavior remains unchanged.

## Tradeoffs

This direction optimizes for adoption, approachability, and cross-functional teams. It is less dense than Field Manual or Signal Room and may feel too informal for highly regulated buyers without a stricter enterprise variant.

## Further Work

If selected, the next pass should extend the work-zone language to onboarding, add collaborative review presence without real-time complexity, and refine empty states with lightweight instructional illustrations.
